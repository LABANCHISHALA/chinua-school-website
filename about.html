About Chinua School

Empowering learners for a brighter future through quality education and modern digital learning tools.

Who We Are

Chinua Primary and Secondary School serves the children of Mpongwe and surrounding communities in the Copperbelt Province of Zambia. We combine dedicated teaching with ICT and digital learning so every learner can succeed.

Our Mission

To deliver quality, accessible education in a safe and modern environment.

Our Vision

Confident, skilled learners who contribute positively to their community and nation.

Our Values

Discipline, respect, integrity, teamwork and a love of learning.
